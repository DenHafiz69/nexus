Nexus is what happens when a developer looks at a long URL and thinks, "This is a personal attack on my screen real estate." It's a lightweight multitool web application designed to solve the two biggest problems in human history: URLs being too long and the world not having enough pixelated squares.

## 🛠️ The "I Can't Believe It's Not Over-Engineered" Stack

- **FastAPI**: Because standard Python wasn't fast enough for our 2 users.
- **PostgreSQL**: Because storing 5-character strings requires the power of a relational database.
- **HTMX & Tailwind**: Making the UI feel modern without the 3GB of `node_modules`.
- **Docker**: So it works on my machine, and now it's your problem too.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📏 The URL Shrinker (Link Shortener)
Does your URL look like a CVS receipt? Our URL shortener puts your links on a strict keto diet. 
- **Before**: `https://www.google.com/search?q=why+is+my+code+not+working+and+why+is+the+internet+so+mean+to+me`
- **After**: `nexus.sh/sad`

### ⬛ The QR Code Obsession (QR Generator)
We transform your boring text into a complex arrangement of black and white squares. It's basically digital modern art that your phone can actually understand.
- **Scanning Tip**: If you squint hard enough, you can see the matrix. (You can't, but it sounds cool).

## 🏗️ How to Get This Thing Running

If you have Docker, you're 90% of the way to digital enlightenment.

```bash
docker-compose up -d --build
```

Now, point your browser to `http://localhost:8000` and witness the majesty. If it doesn't work, have you tried turning it off and on again?
